You are working on the Euro Parking Enforcement System.

OBJECTIVE:
When evidence is uploaded, AI must FIRST ask whether
the evidence has already been reviewed by a human.

AI behaviour must change based on human confirmation.

-----------------------------------
FLOW OVERVIEW
-----------------------------------
1. Evidence is uploaded
2. System asks:
   "Has this evidence already been checked by a human?"
3. Based on answer:
   - YES → AI validates human decision
   - NO  → AI performs full analysis

-----------------------------------
UI CHANGE (MANDATORY)
-----------------------------------
After evidence upload, show a modal:

🧑‍⚖️ Human Review Status

Question:
"Has this evidence already been checked by a human officer?"

Options:
🔘 Yes – Human already reviewed
🔘 No – AI should analyse now

Button:
➡ Continue

-----------------------------------
OPTION 1: YES (Human Reviewed)
-----------------------------------
If user selects YES:

Show secondary question:
"What was the human decision?"

Options:
✔ Evidence Correct (PCN allowed)
✖ Evidence Faulty (PCN issue)

Optional text area:
"Human remarks (optional)"

-----------------------------------
AI BEHAVIOUR (YES PATH)
-----------------------------------
AI MUST NOT re-analyse from scratch.

AI TASK:
- Cross-check AI findings with human decision
- Validate consistency only
- Highlight discrepancies

AI OUTPUT:
- "AI agrees with human assessment"
OR
- "AI found potential mismatch with human assessment"

AI MUST NOT:
- Override human decision
- Auto-change PCN eligibility

-----------------------------------
OPTION 2: NO (AI Analyse)
-----------------------------------
If user selects NO:

AI TASK:
- Perform full evidence analysis
- Extract:
  - Timestamp from image/video
  - Location
  - Vehicle visibility
  - Weather conditions
  - Signage clarity
- Generate confidence score

AI OUTPUT:
- AI analysis report
- Mark status:
  "AI Analysed – Pending Human Review"

-----------------------------------
BACKEND LOGIC
-----------------------------------
Store in evidence_case:

- uploadedAt
- aiAnalysisStatus (pending | completed)
- humanReviewedBeforeAI (true | false)
- humanDecision (valid | faulty | null)
- aiAgreementStatus (agree | disagree | n/a)

-----------------------------------
PCN SAFETY RULES
-----------------------------------
- AI analysis alone cannot issue PCN
- Human confirmation always required
- If human marked faulty → PCN blocked permanently

-----------------------------------
UI STATUS BADGES
-----------------------------------
Show on evidence card:

🧠 AI Analysed – Awaiting Human Review
✔ Human Reviewed – Valid
✖ Human Reviewed – Faulty
🧠 AI vs Human – Discrepancy Found

-----------------------------------
STRICT REQUIREMENTS
-----------------------------------
- Human decision has priority
- AI cannot silently re-analyse
- Every decision must be auditable
- No PCN without human confirmation

Implement clean, modular, production-ready code.
Do not add unrelated features.
