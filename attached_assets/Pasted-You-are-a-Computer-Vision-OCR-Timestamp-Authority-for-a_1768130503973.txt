The system is currently displaying incorrect Observe From / Observe To times.

- DD-MM-YYYY HH:MM
- DD-MM-YYYY HH:MM:SS
- DD/MM/YYYY HH:MM
- YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM
- Tue 30-12-2025 14:32:27

❌ Time only
❌ Date only
❌ Partial / cut-off text
❌ Low-contrast unreadable text
